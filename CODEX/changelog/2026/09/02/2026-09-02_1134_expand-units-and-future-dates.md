# Expand units and future reading dates

- Added a categorized, searchable unit catalog with broader electricity,
  energy, volume, mass, level, and general-purpose meter units.
- Kept relevant units prominent for each meter type while adding a
  `Weitere Einheit` flow and validated custom units up to 20 characters.
- Allowed reading dates through 31 December 2100 and renamed the editable
  timestamp to `Zeitpunkt der Ablesung`.
- Added an explicit warning and confirmation for future timestamps and marks
  them in reading details and newly generated evidence PDFs.
- Added focused domain and widget coverage for custom units, catalog search,
  future-date limits, confirmation, and evidence notices.
